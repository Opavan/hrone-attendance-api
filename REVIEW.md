## Reviewed and not a defect

- `load_dotenv()` does not override existing variables, so real environment variables win, which is what the contract requires.
- `list_employees` excludes `_id` with its projection `{"_id": 0}`; that part is correct.
- `compute_overtime` anchoring the shift end with `tzinfo=IST` on the attendance date is the right idea. Only the threshold and overnight handling were missing (#5, #6).
- Returning 409 for a duplicate punch-in is the right status code. Only the mechanism (check-then-insert) was wrong.
- Comparing `YYYY-MM-DD` strings with `$gte` and `$lte` is valid, because ISO dates sort in date order.
- The punch-in defaults (`history: []`, `half_day: False`, `overtime_minutes: 0`) match DATA_MODEL.md.
- Identifying records by `emp_code` and not by `_id` is correct.
